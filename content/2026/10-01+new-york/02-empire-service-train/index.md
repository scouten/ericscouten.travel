This morning, I returned to the city to start a series of meetings with my team in New York City. I used Amtrak's [Empire Service](https://www.amtrak.com/empire-service-train) train — not to be confused with the Empire _Builder_ which serves the Midwest and Pacific Northwest — and was treated to some wonderful morning fog and sunrise photos of the Hudson River.
