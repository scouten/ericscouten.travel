My cousin recently referred to the changing of the leaves as "garish fall colors." I say bring on the garish!

<!-- more -->

My mom, Lisa, and I flew to New York City last Thursday and visited relatives upstate over the weekend. There were, indeed, garish fall colors to be found on the drive from the city to points north.
