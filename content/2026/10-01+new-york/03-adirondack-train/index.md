My time in the city is done (no photos – sorry). Today I treated myself to a ride on the [Adirondack Train](https://www.amtrak.com/adirondack-train), which mirrors the route of the Empire Service as far as Albany, but then diverges north to Montreal. Since I was returning to visit family upstate, I hopped off the train at Plattsburgh.

I mentioned earlier that we followed the water for 174 km / 108 miles. That journey took 3 hours and 15 minutes, so ... if you're in a hurry to get from Albany to Plattsburgh, this form of travel is probably not for you. But if you want to keep your eyes on the scenery — especially some scenery not accessible by road — enjoy!

I did!
