My wife and I invited my mom to join us for a quiet weekend away to wrap up our summer holidays. We returned to the [Sunshine Coast](/2025/10-04-sunshine-coast/) to revisit an area I had enjoyed last fall.

<!-- more -->

Our arrival to Gibsons was not at _all_ sunny. Our ferry from Vancouver to Gibsons was met with winter-like blustery weather, but that made for some very fun cloudscapes.
